Thai Tea Recipe

Thai tea is a sweet, creamy, and aromatic beverage made from strong black tea, milk, sugar, and often spices, known for its distinctive orange color and rich flavor.

Ingredients

  • Thai Tea Mix
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Half and Half

Steps

  1. Stovetop
  2. 8 cups of water
  3. 2 cups Thai tea mix
  4. 1 ½ cup sugar
  5. Bring to a boil for 15 minutes
  6. Strain the liquid with a straining cloth
  7. Add your Half and Half until the color turns to a bright orange
