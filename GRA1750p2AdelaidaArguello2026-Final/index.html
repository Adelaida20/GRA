Thai Tea Recipe

Thai tea is a sweet, creamy, and aromatic beverage made from strong black tea, milk, sugar, and often spices, known for its distinctive orange color and rich flavor.

Ingredients

  • Thai Tea Mix
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Half and Half
  • Ice Cubes

Steps

  1. Bring 8 cups of water to a rolling boil in a pot
  2. 2 cups Thai tea mix
  3. 1 ½ cup sugar
  4. Reduce the heat and let it simmer for three minutes and then turn off the stove
  5. Let it the tea cool down after simmering for three minutes
  6. Strain the liquid with a straining cloth
  7. Assemble the drink with a tall glass of ice cups, add tea into the glass until it is about three quarters full.
  8. Top with milk of choice, for my drink, I use Half and Half. Pour your milk until it turns into a bright orange
